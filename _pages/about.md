I am a PhD researcher in the hardware group at [COSIC](https://www.esat.kuleuven.be/cosic/), KU Leuven in Leuven, Belgium, supervised by [Ingrid Verbauwhede](https://www.esat.kuleuven.be/cosic/people/person/?u=u0018159) and [Jan-Pieter D'Anvers](https://danversjp.github.io/).
During the summer of 2025, I was a research intern with the Security Circuits team at [Intel Labs](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/research/security.html), Hillsboro (OR), USA.

Before that, I received my MSc in Electrical Engineering (Electronics and Chip Design) and BSc in Engineering Sciences (Electrical Engineering/Computer Science) from KU Leuven, Leuven, Belgium.
During the summer of 2021, I was a research intern with the Hardware Research Group at [Nokia Bell Labs](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/research/), Antwerp, Belgium.

My research is primarily focused on embedded security and post-quantum cryptography (PQC). 
This includes the development of novel countermeasures against physical attacks, optimized software/hardware implementations for embedded and high-end platforms, and formal and practical evaluation of countermeasures.
